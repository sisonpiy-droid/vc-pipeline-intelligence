Data Flow (step by step)

  1. Cron (Mon+Thu 09:30 IST) or a manual trigger starts a run; one queue row per investor.
  2. An invocation claims 3 investors at a time; unfinished work is handed to a fresh invocation.
  3. Research: Tavily search for the last 30 days → fetch each page → read its own publication date.
  4. Validate: investor named? inside the window? dates consistent? duplicate event? else reject with a reason.
  5. Gemini Flash classifies and judges relevance; its quote must exist verbatim in the page.
  6. The scoring engine computes the 0–100 score, action and explanation (deterministic).
  7. One transaction as role vc_refresh: save evidence, coverage and audit; update only the 4 columns.
  8. Failures keep the previous score and are recorded; the run is “succeeded” only when every item is done.
  9. The dashboard derives states at read time; startups reuse the same evidence with their own rubric.
  10. Every morning the Telegram digest summarises it; the founder opens a draft and sends it from their own mail app.

Founder Workflow (who does what)

  1. Open the priority queue: the answer first, each pick with its source, score and opening line.
  2. Preview the email: your pitch, why this investor, and your Calendly link.
  3. Press Send mail (or Gmail): it opens in your own mail app and the card moves to Contacted. Undo is one click.
  4. Replies land in your inbox; log the outcome on the investor page. Follow-up reminders appear on the board and the brief.
  5. Ask the assistant anything about the pipeline; answers come only from your data.

The application never sends email and never reads a mailbox.
